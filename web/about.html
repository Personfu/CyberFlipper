▸ WHAT THIS IS NOW

FLLC's public developer hardware lab.

CyberFlipper is the public content engine for Furulie LLC: a place to publish free hardware-security education, maker documentation, safe lab workflows, and professional service pathways around Flipper Zero, Proxmark, Hak5-style training, DEF CON Badgelife, RF/NFC/IR, tamper-evident practice, and embedded engineering.

FREE EDUCATION

Checklists, build notes, teardown structure, firmware hygiene, and safe starter labs for students, makers, defenders, and conference badge fans.

DEVELOPER ADD-ONS

Reusable docs and assets for Flipper SD-card packs, Proxmark notes, GPIO wiring, IR/NFC/RFID training sets, and badge-life experiments.

FLLC SERVICES

Workshops, documentation cleanup, lab-kit design, defensive workflow buildouts, portfolio projects, and authorized assessment support.

AUTHORIZED_USE_POLICY.txt
[ALLOW] owned devices, lab ranges, CTFs, classrooms, client-approved scopes

PROJECT POSITIONING

  • Audience: students, makers, defenders, hardware hackers, and authorized consultants

  • Repo rule: public pages should reduce risk, not amplify misuse
