CVE RESEARCH WITHOUT MISUSE PATHS

This page turns vulnerability research into a safe triage workflow: understand the issue, identify exposure, prioritize patching, document detection ideas, and keep testing inside authorized labs.

CVE_TRIAGE.playbook
[STEP 1] Verify the advisory through vendor, NVD, CISA KEV, or trusted primary source.
[STEP 2] Identify whether your owned environment has the affected product, version, role, or exposure.
[STEP 3] Rank by exposure, business criticality, exploit maturity, and patch availability.
[STEP 4] Apply patches, compensating controls, segmentation, or configuration hardening.
[STEP 5] Document detection ideas: logs, asset queries, EDR/SIEM searches, and owner follow-up.
[BOUNDARY] Do not generate exploit chains, stealth instructions, persistence, or unauthorized testing steps.
PRIMARY SOURCES
CISA KEVNIST NVDCVE ProgramFree Labs

Use primary references first. Treat social posts and tool output as leads, not final truth.

PATCH PRIORITY CARD

Write one card per vulnerability: asset owner, affected version, exposure, business impact, patch link, compensating control, due date, and verification evidence.

DETECTION CARD

Capture safe detection ideas: log source, event fields, command-line patterns, network indicators from advisories, and known-good exclusions.

LAB VALIDATION CARD

If testing is needed, use isolated lab hosts, sample configs, vendor-safe checks, and written permission. Record rollback and cleanup steps.
