# Safe Scope — 2026-06-20

CyberFlipper daily updates are limited to authorized defensive security research.

## In scope

- Firmware and app change review.
- Defensive release notes.
- Lab safety policy.
- Detection and mitigation guidance.
- Source links and provenance notes.
- Benign local text-entry demonstrations.
- Synthetic fixture documentation.

## Out of scope

- Unauthorized access testing.
- Live third-party device interaction.
- Production card or badge material.
- Credential handling.
- Hidden host actions.
- Destructive behavior.
- Operational instructions for bypassing physical or digital controls.

## Default action

If a workflow could affect a third-party system or expose sensitive material, document the risk and hold it for human review.
